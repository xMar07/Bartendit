Bartendit is a small silly catcher game where you need to catch fire partciles in order to get enough money to buy potions and try out your own cocktails (game still in development)
in the main minigame you are supposed to move the cauldron to collect blue fire particles that are necessary to make the potions , the more u catch them the higher score u get and the more coins u gain , some coins will happen to spawn from time to time make sure to catch them , dont catch the red particles though otherwise your health bar will decrease 
visit the Potion Bar in order to buy potions and make your very own cocktails and test them out and see the effects 
(game still under development)
